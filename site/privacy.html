Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This page describes Kol's Korner, the site at koltregaskes.com. Kol Tregaskes also runs these separate sites:

  • The AI Resource Hub - theairesourcehub.com
  • Axy Lusion - axylusion.com
  • Ghost in the Models - ghostinthemodels.com
  • Kol Tregaskes Photography - koltregaskesphotography.com

This policy does not describe how those sites handle visitors.

What Kol's Korner collects

Kol's Korner does not run analytics. It does not count page views, record which site sent you here, store IP addresses, or build a profile of visitors. There is no analytics tool attached to this site.

There is no account, no newsletter signup, and no contact form, so the site does not collect your email address.

Cookies and data stored in your browser

Kol's Korner does not set tracking cookies. The news page can remember articles you save or flag, and the site can remember whether you chose a light or dark theme. That information stays in your browser. It is not sent to Kol's Korner.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep ordinary server logs for that hosting, such as the page requested and the address the request came from. Those logs belong to GitHub's hosting service. They are not an analytics tool added to this site. GitHub's privacy policy covers that infrastructure.

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com to fetch the font files. Kol's Korner does not receive that request. Google's own privacy policy applies to it.

The site does not use Google Analytics, a Facebook pixel, or any other tracking script. Links to YouTube, Suno, and other sites are ordinary links. This site does not embed their players.

External links

Kol's Korner links to external websites. Kol is not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Contact and newsletters

Kol's Korner does not collect email addresses. The newsletter has not launched, and the contact form is not available. A message sent on X is handled by X, not stored by this site.

Your rights under UK data protection law

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)

On this site there is usually nothing to send, correct, or delete, because there is no account, email list, or analytics profile. You can still use the contact link below.

Children

Kol's Korner is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the last revision.

Contact
